# Editorial artwork prompts

The three illustrations in this directory were generated with Codex's built-in image generation tool for this portfolio. The PNG outputs were resized and encoded as WebP for the website.

## editorial-governance.webp

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: editorial portfolio artwork for an AI governance feature
Primary request: a sophisticated old-world broadsheet illustration that visualizes responsible AI governance at a telecommunications company
Scene/backdrop: an architectural arrangement of paper ledgers, index cards, approval stamps, branching decision paths, and a subtle telecommunications tower silhouette, all as symbolic editorial imagery
Style/medium: richly detailed wood engraving mixed with hand-inked digital illustration, 19th-century newspaper etching, crisp print texture
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2; strong central visual and breathing room at edges; no frame or border
Color palette: warm parchment #e2dedb, near-black ink #1d1d1b, restrained single ember orange #c03f13 stamp accent
Constraints: no text, letters, numbers, logos, real people, watermark, gradients, blur, UI screenshot, or misleading charts
```
